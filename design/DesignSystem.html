01 · Brand

Voice & principles

How Ayooda speaks across product, marketing, and conversation.

Tone

Warm. Direct. Slightly understated.

Speak like a competent colleague who respects the reader’s time. Avoid hype words. Never cute.

Headlines

Two clauses. One italic.

Pair a plain statement with an italicized accent phrase set in the brand color. Keeps copy editorial without shouting.

Numerals

Always specific.

“Up to 60% of tickets” beats “most tickets.” Use tabular figures in product UI; oldstyle figures in long copy.

06 · Patterns

Section anatomy

Every marketing section follows the same skeleton: eyebrow → display headline (with one italic accent phrase) → lead paragraph → content. Spacing is 100px vertical between sections.

◆  Eyebrow

Plain statement, then an italic accent phrase.

07 · Iconography

Glyph blocks, not icons

Stroked geometric SVGs sit inside a 44px tinted square. 1.4px stroke. One filled accent dot per icon, max. No gradient icons, no emoji.

08 · Logo

09 · Guardrails

Don’t

  • Don’t introduce a second accent color. The amber does all primary work.
  • Don’t stack two italic phrases in a single headline. One accent phrase per heading.
  • Don’t use emoji as iconography. Use stroked SVG glyph blocks.
  • Don’t mix shadow recipes. Soft for hover, card for elevation — that’s it.
  • Don’t set body type below 14px outside dense product UI.
