Ayooda logo variants

Six directions on the Linear dark palette. Each card shows the mark large, as a nav lockup, and at 32px and 16px.

Ayooda

1 · Orbit

Keeps the current ring-and-core idea but fixes it: the ring opens where the agent "pings" out. Reads as always-on, a signal leaving the core. Simple enough for a 16px favicon.

Ayooda

2 · Monogram A

A geometric A built from one stroke. The crossbar is replaced by a lime dot: the "A" for Ayooda and the AI agent, answering at the centre of every conversation.

Ayooda

3 · Aperture tile

A dark app-icon tile with a hairline border, matching the Linear system. Ring plus core stays recognisable, and the tile gives the widget avatar, favicon and social cards one shape.

Ayooda

4 · Handoff

Two overlapping circles, AI and human, with the lime overlap as the hand-off. It is the product promise in one shape: autonomy with accountability.

Ayooda

5 · Lime bubble

The boldest option: a solid lime chat bubble with a dark A. High recall as a widget launcher or app icon, and it makes the one brand colour the logo itself.

Ayooda

6 · Double-o

Plays on the double o in Ayooda: two circles, a customer and an agent, side by side, one filled lime. Works as a mark on its own and sits naturally inside the wordmark.
