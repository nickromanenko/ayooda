# Team members and invitations

The Team page lets an owner invite people who will help operate the workspace. Team membership and agent access are separate: joining the workspace does not automatically grant a member access to every agent.

## Invite a teammate

Enter the teammate's existing or intended sign-in email and choose **Invite**. Ayooda creates a link that you can copy and share securely. The person must sign up or sign in with the invited email for the invitation to match.

Until the invitation is accepted, it appears under **Pending invites**. Use **Revoke** if the recipient should no longer be able to join. Revoking invalidates the pending invitation but does not affect an already accepted member.

## Roles and agent access

The member list shows each person's display name, email, and workspace role. Owners manage workspace-level settings, billing, channels, credentials, members, and agent access. Members focus on permitted agents, Copilot, and Inbox work.

After a new member joins, open each relevant agent's Security page and enable access. This lets the member configure or use that agent according to the current product permissions. Grant only the access needed for the person's role.

## Remove a member

Use the remove control beside a non-owner member to revoke their workspace membership. This does not delete customer conversations or internal notes they created. The workspace owner cannot be removed through this control.

If an invite fails, verify the email format and whether the person is already a member or has a pending invite. If the Team page cannot load, retry before making changes; a load failure does not modify membership.
