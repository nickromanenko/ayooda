# Billing and plans

The Billing page shows the workspace subscription, current-period usage, available plans, and projected conversation overage. Billing controls are available to workspace owners.

## Current plan and service state

The current-plan card can show a paid plan, free trial, no plan, past-due payment, or paused service. During a trial it shows the approximate number of days remaining. **Manage billing** opens the secure billing portal for payment methods, invoices, and subscription management.

If service is paused, restore an entitled subscription or resolve the billing issue in the portal. Existing workspace configuration and conversation history are not erased by a billing interruption.

## Included conversations and overage

The usage bar compares current-period conversations with the plan's included allowance. The allowance is shared by all agents in the workspace. When usage exceeds the allowance, the page shows the overage conversation count and an estimated cost using the displayed per-conversation rate.

The figure is an in-period estimate, not a final invoice. Use individual agent Usage pages to understand which agents contribute to the total.

## Change a plan

Plan cards show the monthly price and included conversation allowance. Choose or switch a plan with its action button. The current plan is marked and cannot be selected again. A successful checkout returns to Billing and may take a short time to appear while the payment provider confirms it.

If Billing cannot load, use **Retry**. Avoid repeating checkout actions while a payment is still processing. If a completed payment does not appear after refreshing, inspect the billing portal before starting another checkout.
